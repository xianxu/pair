## Decisions & dead ends

**Rules that came out of six review rounds. These are the deliverable.**

- An irreversible step never precedes a revocable check, and its precondition is
  proved about **the exact entity the step acts on**.
- A guard omitted as "unrepresentable" must cite the validator clause that makes
  it so, **read including its exceptions**, and be pinned by a test that builds
  the fixture through the real store. This claim was made twice and was wrong
  twice (`threadrecord/lifecycle.go`'s `replacementUnknown` escape).
- Write a sweep as a **predicate**, not a list of sites. "Every guard refusing on
  `record.Incarnations` or `record.Park`" written as four sites let two further
  shapes through.
- When a value's **meaning** changes, enumerate every reader and re-derive each
  in the same round.
- Guidance belongs at the **consumer** that needs it, not as a marker every
  producer carries. Forcing every producer to carry a `ResumeDiagnosticCode`
  changed what the code *meant* and broke `errors.As`/`Is`/`Unwrap`.
- A vocabulary needs a **produced-by guard**; a value nothing emits is a branch
  no test can reach.
- A **diagnostic code is a claim** the operator reads. Never emit "not running"
  where only ignorance was established.
- A claim restated away from its test is how it comes to be wrong. Don't restate
  the model in the plan or the atlas; point at the tested source.
- **A table asserting totality over a domain takes its dimensions from the
  domain's own oracle, not the author's enumeration.** Same rule as "a predicate,
  not a list of sites", applied to the test rather than the code:
  `TestReAdoptionExitsAreTotalAndCoded` now loops wider than any enumerated shape
  and lets `CreateThread`'s refusal skip what is out of domain — 32 cells, 6
  skipped, up from 13 hand-written.

## Lessons learned

- **Never pipe `make test` into `head`/`tail`** — SIGPIPE reports a phantom
  failure with no failing package. Redirect to a file. This cost two false
  diagnoses in this session.
- `ps` is blocked by the agent sandbox; a `|| echo` fallback silently turns the
  denial into "DEAD". Use `kill -0` (ESRCH vs EPERM) or disable the sandbox
  deliberately.
- Mutation-check every guard you claim is pinned. Two tests here passed while
  their subject was inverted, and one *table* passed because it checked
  self-consistency rather than which cells may act.
- Commit messages containing quotes break `git commit -m`; use `-F` with a file.
- `TestParkCoordinatorConstructorDoesNotQueryPairSession` flaked once in a full
  run ("New blocked before returning") and passes at base, in isolation, and on
  re-run. Timing-sensitive around `couch.New`; look again if it recurs.
