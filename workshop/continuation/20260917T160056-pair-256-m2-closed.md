## Lessons learned

- **Never pipe `make test` into `head`/`tail`** — SIGPIPE reports a phantom
  failure with no failing package. Redirect to a file.
- `ps` is blocked by the agent sandbox; use `kill -0` (ESRCH vs EPERM) or disable
  the sandbox deliberately. Many pty tests also fail only on the sandbox
  (`operation not permitted`) — not regressions.
- `$TMPDIR` is unset under `dangerouslyDisableSandbox`, so `> "$TMPDIR/x.log"`
  silently writes to `/x.log` and the log vanishes. Use an absolute scratch path.
- Mutation-check every guard you claim is pinned. The reviewer found two of mine
  that were not, by deleting them and watching the tests stay green.
